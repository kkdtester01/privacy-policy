Privacy Policy — SystemCare

Publisher: GreenCube · Effective date: 5 October 2026 · Contact: kkd.backup01@gmail.com

Summary. SystemCare analyses your device on your device. Your app list, usage statistics, storage contents, and hardware details are processed locally and are never uploaded by us. The free version contains ads served by Google AdMob, which involves Google's own data processing as described below. The Pro version contains no ads, no ad SDK, and no network permission at all.

1. Who we are

SystemCare (“the app”) is published by GreenCube (“we”, “us”). Questions or requests about this policy or your data: kkd.backup01@gmail.com.

2. What the app processes — and where

The app reads the following information locally on your device only. None of it is transmitted to us, because we operate no servers and collect no analytics:

  • Installed apps — names, package identifiers, install/update times, APK and cache sizes (used for the App Manager and storage breakdown).
  • Usage statistics — only if you grant the special “Usage access” permission: foreground time and last-used times per app (used for unused-app insights). This permission is optional and the related features degrade gracefully without it.
  • Storage metadata — file names, sizes, types and modification dates of media/documents via Android's MediaStore (used for large-file and duplicate-file finders). The app never reads file contents except short sample hashes needed to verify duplicate files, and those hashes exist only in memory during the scan.
  • Device vitals — battery level/health, CPU utilisation, memory, and temperature readings (Performance, Battery, Dashboard screens).
  • Network counters — byte totals per network type read from the operating system's traffic stats. The app itself does not transmit your usage data.
  • Security settings — whether a screen lock, biometrics, or USB debugging are enabled (Security check screen). Read-only, on-device.

Scan results, scores, and history are stored in the app's private, on-device database and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

3. Data we do NOT do

  • We do not operate any backend servers.
  • We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.
  • We do not include any analytics or crash-reporting SDK.
  • We do not access the content of your messages, contacts, or location.

4. Ads (free version only)

The free version displays ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland/Google LLC). When you use the free version, Google may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP, and device characteristics in order to select and serve ads. This is handled by Google under Google's Privacy & Terms.

  • You can reset or opt out of personalised advertising in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Ad loading requires the INTERNET permission in the free version. That permission exists solely for the ad SDK — the app's analysis features remain fully offline-capable.

The Pro version contains no advertising, no AdMob SDK, and no INTERNET permission.

5. Permissions the app may request

  • Usage access (special permission) — optional; powers app-usage insights.
  • Photos & videos / Music & audio / Storage — optional; powers the large-file and duplicate finders. Only requested when you open those features.
  • Notifications — optional; used for rare, useful alerts (scan results, weekly summary) you enable in Settings.
  • Query all packages — required to list installed apps for the App Manager.
  • Internet (free only) — required by the AdMob SDK.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is rated for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, on or off device.

7. Your rights and choices

Because we hold no server-side data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on your behalf. To remove everything the app has stored: uninstall the app, or use Android Settings → Apps → SystemCare → Storage → Clear data. For questions or complaints, email kkd.backup01@gmail.com and we will respond promptly.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will also be reflected in the app's release notes.

9. Google Play data disclosures

Consistent with the above: the app does not collect or share personal data; the only third-party processing is Google AdMob's ad serving in the free version; all diagnostics are performed on-device; data stored on device (scan history) is never transmitted.
